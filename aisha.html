Why are donations rejected?

Aisha Khan

An object can be unwanted by its owner and still be useful, but someone has to establish that. Missing parts, damage, cleanliness, safety concerns or a lack of storage may make a donation unsuitable. I also have to consider whether there is a realistic next user. Refusing something is not necessarily a refusal to care; accepting it without a usable route can simply move the disposal problem to us.
