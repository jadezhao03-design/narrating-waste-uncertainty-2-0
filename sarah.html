Who verifies the final outcome?

Sarah Green

I would ask what outcome is being claimed and what evidence follows the material through each handover. Collection records show collection; shipment records show movement. Neither alone proves final recovery. Operators, public bodies and independent checks may each hold parts of the picture, but I cannot name a verifier for an unspecified case. The gap between those pieces of evidence is part of the story.
