Why use mixed materials?

Daniel Price

Different layers can do different jobs: one may provide strength, another a barrier against moisture, and another a surface for printing. Combining them can help protect the product, but it can also make separation harder later. I have to weigh those trade-offs against cost and production limits. That does not excuse a difficult design; it means a convenient package can transfer work to the people who discard and process it.
