Why export recyclable material?

Liam Carter

Material may be traded beyond its original area when processing capacity, a buyer's specification and market demand make that route viable. That does not establish that a particular object from Moray is exported, or tell us where it would go. I can explain a documented shipment more readily than an individual household item. Moving material to a buyer is a handover, not proof that recycling has been completed.
