Does clean paper guarantee recycling?

Paper Stack

Being clean helps, but it does not give us a guaranteed destination. We may contain different grades, coatings or attached materials that are not obvious when we sit in one stack. Someone still has to assess us within the collection and processing route available to them. We do not know which mill, if any, will receive us or what product we might become. We look ready to leave the household; that is not the same as having completed recycling.
