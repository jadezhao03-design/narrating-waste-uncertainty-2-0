You

What must collection crews handle?

Mark Evans

People see a bin being emptied; I see the lifting, access, weather and judgement needed to do it safely. A split bag, a heavy container or mixed material can turn a routine stop into extra handling. I cannot know why each household made a mistake, and confusing packaging does not help. The crew is one part of the system, not a machine that can correct every decision made before collection.
