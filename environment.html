What remains invisible to people?

Environment

A cleared kitchen and an emptied bin make a change in what people can see. They do not reveal the later movement of materials, the work of processing them or the residues left behind. Some consequences may be delayed or displaced elsewhere. I cannot tell you that this particular object reached a river, soil or coastline, and I do not treat every discarded thing as the same harm. I draw attention to what a tidy endpoint can leave outside the frame.
