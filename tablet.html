Broken Tablet

Broken does not tell you everything about me. I may contain a usable part, a repairable fault or materials worth recovering, but someone has to inspect me before making that judgement. Repair, reuse and material recovery are different possibilities, not promises. My visible damage does not reveal the condition of every component, and I cannot tell you whether a repair would be practical. Deciding that you no longer want me starts another assessment; it does not finish it.
