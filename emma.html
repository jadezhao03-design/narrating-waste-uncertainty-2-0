Why is sorting so confusing?

Emma Reid

I thought the recycling symbol would settle it, but it does not tell me whether my local collection accepts that particular package. Then there are lids, labels and bits of food to think about, often while I am clearing up in a hurry. I can check the current council guidance, but I cannot redesign the packaging or make more storage space appear. The work of interpreting the system lands in my kitchen.
