Plastic Bottle

I could be collected and sorted, but I cannot promise you the next step. Someone has to identify my material and decide whether my condition fits the route available to them. If I am suitable, I may move towards reprocessing; if I am not, my route may change. You have not told me my material type or where I was placed, so I cannot name a destination. The bin is the beginning of a possible journey, not evidence of its ending.
